---
name: Mobile app top bar
description: Product decision for the mobile app header and notification access.
---

The app home header must remain a single row with search on the left, the days filter between, and the profile avatar on the right. Do not restore a separate profile/notification row or a standalone notification bell.

Notifications belong in the panel opened from the profile avatar, below the profile entry. New notifications are indicated by an unread badge on the avatar.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose a compact single-row header to preserve map space and combine profile and notification access.

**How to apply:** Keep search, date filtering, profile navigation, notification data, and unread state functional when changing the mobile app header. Other pages may retain a back button and title, with profile access on the right.
