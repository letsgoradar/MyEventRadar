---
name: User-location radar marker
description: Visual constraint for changes to the user's location marker on the event map.
---

Keep the original broad rotating radar sweep, range circle, and green pulsing center intact. Extra brand details may be layered over the center, but must never replace or hide the sweep.

**Why:** The user explicitly prefers the original radar animation and rejected a replacement marker without the sweep.

**How to apply:** When changing the user-location marker, preserve its existing radar layers and add any pin, icon, depth, or shadow as a separate centered foreground layer.
